### **Eligibility**

This tournament is solely for teams composing of at least 3/5 players that are citizens or residents of the following countries:

- United Kingdom *(England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland)*
- Ireland
- Iceland
- Isle of Man
- Jersey
- Guernsey

Any remaining players must be playing from countries within [EU Region](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/main/model/util/region.js).

### **Qualification**

- Upon signing up to a qualifier, the five players selected as the starting line-up will be considered the Primary Team if the team qualifies.
- If a team requires a stand-in for a qualifier, the stand-in must be registered as one of the team’s two substitutes and must not be listed among the starting five players.

### **VRS Direct Invite Information**

- Invitations utilise the Valve Regional Standings for Europe as of `5th October 2026` .
- Four (4) teams receive direct invitations to the Online Main Stage. Invitations will start `5th October 2026` .
- Invitations start from 1st place in the Europe VRS standings using the country filters United Kingdom, Ireland & Iceland, provided the team is not ranked in the Top 12 globally.
- If a team is Top 12 globally, the invite passes to the next eligible seed.
- Teams have 72 hours to accept an invite, or it is sent to the next seed down.
- If eligible VRS teams are lower than available slots, UKIC reserves the right to reallocate unfilled slots to Open Qualifiers.
- Invited rosters must field at least three (3) of their original five invited players for the entirety of every event match. Failure to meet this requirement will result in an automatic match forfeit per Valve TOR 3.10.1.

### Seeding

Seeding across all tournament stages follows these metrics in order

1. Valve Regional Standings for Europe as of `5th October 2026`
2. UKIC Season 11 Placements
3. ESEA S58 Average Placements
4. Average FACEIT ELO

> **Note:** In accordance with Valve TOR 3.4.3, all rosters holding an official Global VRS rank will automatically be seeded above unranked rosters across all tournament stages, regardless of secondary metrics.

### **Invite Exceptions & Disqualification Criteria**

In accordance with Section 5 of Valve’s Tournament Operation Requirements, Direct VRS Invites will be issued strictly by rank order, except where a roster meets one or more of the following objective disqualification criteria:

1. **Anti-Cheat & Game Bans:** Any roster containing a player with an active Valve Anti-Cheat (VAC) ban or a confirmed ban on 3rd Party platforms *(FACEIT / Akros / Esportal / Challengermode etc)* will be ineligible.
2. **Integrity Sanctions:** Any roster containing a player or coach currently serving an active suspension issued by the Esports Integrity Commission (ESIC) or listed on official sanctions databases.
3. **Conflict of Interest / Multi-Team Ownership:** Rosters sharing majority ownership, common control, or financial interests with another confirmed participating roster in the same event will be disqualified.
4. **Documentation & Visa Deadlines:** Invited rosters must submit required operational documents (passport copies, visa approvals, and media releases) if required by a player to enter the United Kingdom by `13th October 2026` . Failure to meet this deadline will result in the immediate forfeiture of the invite, which will pass to the next eligible VRS rank or Open Qualifier spot. 
5. **Mid-Tournament Disqualifications:** Rules regarding mid-tournament conduct, match forfeits, technical exploits, and in-competition integrity violations are detailed in our official [Rulebook](https://ukicircuit.com/rulebooks). All mid-tournament disqualifications will be enforced strictly in accordance with the published rulebook penalties.

> **Note:** In the event that a roster is disqualified under these published criteria, a public statement detailing the specific rule breach will be published at the time of the decision.
>
